Projects

Alshare' is a visual documentation foundation working since 2011. It starts from journalism, moves toward investigation, and keeps what it documents as archive and memory.

01

Journalism

the foundation

Alshare's work began in 2011 as journalism from inside Syrian cities, extending the work of a group of journalists in the drought displacement camps in 2010. The team covered protests and daily life through field filming, and produced reports and documentaries for Arab and international channels. That start set the rules that still govern everything the foundation produces: being present in the field, verifying sources, and listening to testimony. The other layers of the work rest on this foundation.

02

Visual documentation

the core of the work

The image is the central tool in everything Alshare' produces: short films and documentaries, interviews, and the documentation of places. The tools have changed with the need: from the camera and the mobile phone to drones, 3D cameras and digital models that reconstruct space. The aim stays the same: to record places and events precisely enough that they can be returned to and verified. Documentary production continues today, including interviews, films and filmed investigations for the museum platforms.

03

Investigation

the direction

Since 2017, the search for missing people has led Alshare's team to buildings used as prisons and detention centres. Investigating prisons, their spaces and the fate of detainees has since become a long-term line of work. The method gathers and connects three elements: the document found on site, the testimony of those who passed through, and the place itself, recorded with 3D cameras and drones. Alshare's work extended from Syria to Iraq. The ISIS Prisons Museum and the Syria Prisons Museum grew out of it as platforms that publish its findings.

04

Archives and the writing of memory

What Alshare' documents does not end with publication. The foundation organizes, describes and preserves what it has filmed and collected since 2011, so that it becomes a visual archive of Syria and the region. Description follows a shared model that records each item’s date, place and rights. The aim is to preserve this material as part of the memory of Syria and the region.

05

Exhibitions and events

a permanent line

Public presentation has accompanied Alshare's work from the start. The foundation has taken the image into squares, damaged buildings and exhibition halls, from celebrations in Syrian cities and a festival of films shot on phones to exhibitions in international museums and cultural institutions. Current exhibitions present the findings of the prisons investigation through image, testimony and virtual reality. The aim is for documented material to reach a wider public.

06

Training and capacity building

present in every layer

Training has been present in every layer of the work, from the beginning to today. In Syria and Iraq, Alshare' has trained photographers, journalists and researchers in filming, directing, documentation and investigative journalism, and has supported the production of their work with equipment and mentoring. It has worked with local civil society organizations and media to build their capacity in media and documentation. The aim is for the tools of documentation to stay in the hands of those who live in the place.
