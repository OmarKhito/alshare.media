Alshare' is a visual documentation foundation working since 2011. It starts from journalism, moves toward investigation, and keeps what it documents as archive and memory.

  • JournalismPresence in the field, verification, listening to testimony.
  • Visual documentationFrom the camera and the mobile phone to 3D cameras.
  • InvestigationA long-term investigation into prisons, their spaces and the fate of detainees.
  • Archives and the writing of memoryOrganizing, describing and preserving what has been filmed and collected since 2011.
  • Exhibitions and eventsPresenting work in public space since the beginning.
  • Training and capacity buildingPhotographers, journalists and researchers in Syria and Iraq.
