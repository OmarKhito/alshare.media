FRENعربي

A visual and documentary production foundation since 2011.

Alshare' began in 2011 by covering the Syrian revolution from inside its cities, and by producing reports and documentaries. It continues to build a visual archive of Syria and the region that documents events and places and digitally reconstructs sites.

Alshare' produces films and presents the image in public space, through celebrations in city squares and festivals that have screened work produced inside and outside Syria.

Alshare' documents sites through 360-degree photography and drones, gathers testimonies tied to place, and produces three-dimensional models that reconstruct it.

Alshare' has trained hundreds of journalists, filmmakers, designers, artists and visual creators in photography, directing and filmmaking, and has collaborated with them on producing work.
