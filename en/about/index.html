About

Alshare' is a visual and documentary production foundation. Since 2011 it has worked at the intersection of photography, filmmaking, festivals, archive building and investigative spatial documentation. Founded in Syria by journalists and activists it holds a visual archive of Syria and the region that runs from field reporting to spatial reconstruction.

Alshare's work began with coverage of the Syrian revolution and the production of reports and documentaries for Arab and international broadcasters. It then moved into public space, with celebrations in city squares and film festivals that screened work made inside and outside Syria. Over time its tools widened: 360-degree and drone recording of sites, testimonies recorded in the places where events happened, the collection of documents, and the reconstruction of spaces in 3D. Each stage built on the one before it: from the camera in the street, to the square, to the place itself.

Over these years, Alshare' has trained hundreds of journalists, filmmakers, designers, artists and visual creators in photography, directing and filmmaking, and has worked alongside them.

Alshare's practice rests on a single method: go to the place, gather image, document and testimony, verify them, and build from them a narrative and an archive that can be returned to. On that method it works today in documentary production, spatial documentation, and the building of visual archives and access to them.

Alshare' is registered in Canada as Alshare' Media Foundation.
